TNT tier textures sourced from Modrinth resource packs (for MiniArena tower tiers):
- normal  <- Magex's Barrel TNT (magexs-barrel-tnt)
- strong  <- TNT Rust (tnt-rust)
- super   <- Red TNT Barrel (red-tnt-barrel)
- mega    <- Nuclear TNT (nuclear-tnt)
- ultra   <- Black TNT (black-tnt)

Original authors retain their rights; used as textures in this server pack.

Casino textures (cards, chips, roulette pockets, arrow buttons) are original
work generated for MiniArena - no third-party assets. See the generator in
the project history if they need regenerating at a different size or palette.

- card_<rank>_<suit>  52 playing cards, 16x16, corner rank + suit pip
- card_back           face-down card for the dealer's hole card
- chip_<amount>       bet chips, coloured by the standard casino ramp
                      (white 100, red 500, green 1k, black 5k, purple 25k)
- chip_locked         greyed chip for a bet the player cannot afford
- pocket_<colour>     roulette result tiles
- btn_higher/lower    Higher-or-Lower guess arrows
